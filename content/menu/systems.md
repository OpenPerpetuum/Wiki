# Systems

The systems that build you up and move your value: what your robots carry, how
items are made, and how goods and credits flow.

## Industry & progress

Building and upgrading: robots, what they carry, and how your character and gear grow.

- **[Robots & fitting](/features/robots/)** — Selecting your active robot, fitting modules and ammo, presets, and tints.
- **[Production](/features/production/)** — Turning components into items: production lines, refine, repair, reprocess, prototypes, and insurance.
- **[Research](/features/research/)** — The two progression systems: the extension tree (character skills) and the tech tree (unlocks bought with research).
- **[Items & inventory](/features/items/)** — Your inventory: containers, packing, stacking, renaming, and special items like redeemables and goodie packs.
- **[Modules & fitting](/features/modules/)** — The module families (weapons, armor, energy, electronics, EWar, EnWar, industrial, NEXUS) and the rules of thumb.
- **[Sparks](/features/sparks/)** — The nanobot manifestation of your agent — passive ability bonuses, authorization, and switching cost.

## Trade & economy

Moving value: the market, the item shop, and shipping between bases.

- **[Market & trade](/features/market/)** — The economy: market orders, direct purchases, direct player trades, the item shop, and credits.
- **[Transport](/features/transport/)** — Shipping items between bases: submitting, taking, delivering, retrieving, and the reward/collateral economy.
- **[Item shop](/content/shop/)** — The fixed-price vendor catalog, split by category: what each vendor sells and at what price.
