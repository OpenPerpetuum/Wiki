# Systems

The systems that build you up and move your value: what your robots carry, how
items are made, and how goods and credits flow.

## Build & grow

- **[Robots & fitting](/features/robots/)** — Selecting your active robot, fitting modules and ammo, presets, and tints.
- **[Production](/features/production/)** — Turning components into items: production lines, refine, repair, reprocess, prototypes, and insurance. Full tables: [Recipes](/content/recipes/).
- **[Research](/features/research/)** — The progression bought with research: the [tech tree](/content/techtree/) of per-character and per-corp unlocks.
- **[Character](/features/character/)** — The character window: the [extensions](/content/extensions/) skill tree and [sparks](/features/sparks/).

## Carry & trade

- **[Items & inventory](/features/items/)** — Your inventory: containers, packing, stacking, renaming, and special items like redeemables and goodie packs. Also: [Modules & fitting](/features/modules/) — the module families and the rules of thumb.
- **[Market & trade](/features/market/)** — The economy: market orders, direct purchases, direct player trades, and credits. Also: [Transport](/features/transport/) (shipping between bases) and the [Item shop](/content/shop/) (fixed-price vendor catalog).
