# Reference

Generated data for the whole game, plus the background: stat tables, catalogs,
zones, and the jargon. (The setting's story lives in the [World](/zones/map/) section's [Lore](/features/lore/).)

- **[Content](/content/)** — Generated stat tables for every content entity: ores, plants, deployables, robots. (Items have their own pages, reached by search or from the pages that reference them.)
- **[Zones](/zones/)** — How zone resources are generated: ore nodes, plant populations, and worked examples of real zones.
- **[All features](/features/)** — Everything a player can do in the game, organized by system.
- **[Abbreviations](/features/abbreviations/)** — The jargon: EP, NIC, CT, TP, EWar and the rest of the shorthand players use.
- **[Server & reference](/features/server/)** — Server info, high scores, reference data downloads, and the in-game store.
- **[Formats (dev)](/formats/)** — File-format reference for developers: zone files and content fields.
